# What to avoid: our first series

Our first Reels series did poorly. Use it as the example of what not to do.
Each of these probably hurt, and each is checked by a gate:

1. **A black title card at the start** (2.2 seconds). The first second is
   when people decide to scroll past. Gate: stage 3, no title card; the
   first frame already shows the action.
2. **Two minutes long.** Most Reels that hold attention are 30 to 60 seconds.
   Gate: stage 4 and stage 6, 30 to 60 s.
3. **Every part depends on the one before.** Part 5 makes no sense to someone
   who never saw Part 1. Gate: stage 2 one-sentence test read cold, and
   stage 4 standalone check (no "part", "episode", "last time", "previously").
4. **A text-to-speech voice that sounds like it.** Gate: stage 5's voice
   score on our rubric; the voice is decided there.
5. **Judged on continuity instead of retention.** Both matter; we only ever
   checked continuity. Gate: stage 4 three-second slices, and stage 7 judges
   by 3-second hold, watch % and shares, not by how well it fits the series.

**Series, since 2026-11-23.** Thomas asked for an 8-part story series with an
outro that names the next part ("Part 2 tomorrow!") and 1 to 2 minutes a part.
For series parts only, that replaces points 2 and 3 above: the length gate is
60 to 120 s, and the outro may say the next part. Each part is still a complete
story read cold, with no "previously" and no part number anywhere else
(`pipeline/series/REQUIREMENTS.md`).
