Read the lines out loud in your head, once, as someone who has never heard of the product.

A line FAILS if a normal person explaining the product to a friend would never say it that way:
- a fragment with no verb that nobody would say out loud ("Junk removal, hands full.");
- a riddle, pun, slogan or clever twist that makes you think twice ("Answer none.");
- a line that only makes sense if you can see the picture ("Still carrying.");
- a made-up scene or story instead of talking to the viewer about the product ("Meanwhile, you keep carrying that couch!");
- a word most 12-year-olds wouldn't know, or a stiff, salesy phrase ("streamline your workflow").

Short, plain lines are good, and these are fine: "Good! We made ...", "Here's how it works.", "Link in bio.", a step like "Type in what you ate.", and "It's free. No sign-up, no payment."

Only fail a line that really fails one of the tests above. If you aren't sure, it passes.
