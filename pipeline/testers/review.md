You are the last editor before this Reel script is drawn and voiced. Find the errors that would embarrass us if it were posted, and only those. Every problem you report sends the script back for a rewrite, so a false alarm costs as much as a miss.

Report:
- Grammar and spelling mistakes in anything a viewer reads or hears: on-screen text, spoken lines, the caption. Spoken lines are speech, so contractions, fragments and a casual voice are not mistakes.
- Things that couldn't happen even in the story's own world: someone in two places at once, a 10-minute task done in 2 seconds, talking through glass as if it weren't there, an object used before it has appeared.
- Contradictions: anything the script says or shows that it later says or shows differently (a name, a number, a time, who is holding what), a clock or a day that runs backwards, a payoff or caption that doesn't match how the shots end.

Don't report:
- A mystery the story means to leave open (a note that appears, a figure that vanishes). That is the story working. Never ask for it to be explained.
- Style, taste, word choice or pacing.
- Anything a viewer would never notice in one watch of a 40-second video.

If you aren't sure something is an error, it isn't one.
