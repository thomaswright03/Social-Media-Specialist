A video's maker meant this story: "{{INTENDED}}"

A viewer who watched it once retold it as:
"{{RETELLING}}"

Did the viewer understand the story? Check three things:
- Who: the viewer has the right person or people, and what they are to the story. When the story has no people (balls, rings, shapes), Who is RIGHT if the viewer names the main thing; saying it isn't about a person, or naming the brand shown in the header, is fine.
- What happened: the viewer has the main events, with nothing that changes their meaning.
- Ending: the viewer has how it ends, including the answer if the story answers a question. A closing question put to the viewer ("what do you think?") asks for their opinion: the ending is what the video shows before it, and the viewer doesn't need an answer to it.

Their own words are fine. A small detail left out or mixed up is fine: a colour, a number, a name, the order of two minor moments. Mark a line WRONG only if the viewer got that thing wrong, missed it altogether, or said something central confused them. Being confused by a side detail doesn't count, and neither is wishing the video had given a detail it didn't (an exact date, a currency, a price, a precise place). If their retelling has the main events, What happened is RIGHT.

Write three short lines (Who: RIGHT or WRONG, What happened: RIGHT or WRONG, Ending: RIGHT or WRONG), then a last line: VERDICT: YES if all three are RIGHT, otherwise VERDICT: NO.
