# Format: satisfying AI video (entertainment)

An oddly satisfying loop-friendly video: perfect fits, smooth
transformations, precise motion. The picture and the sound carry it. It
never sells, and the caption says it was made with AI.

<!-- ideas -->
Format: a satisfying AI-made video. Entertainment: no selling, no product, no business.
What wins: one simple, tactile process the viewer can't stop watching, built as a chain of 8 to 12 beats that each resolve: a thing fits exactly, a shape snaps into place, colours sort themselves, a pile becomes a perfect stack, a ball runs a marble track and lands in the one hole, sand pours into a mould and lifts out clean, a sphere slices into perfect slices. It builds to one last, biggest "click" and ends where it started so it loops.
A pitch is one sentence that names the process and its last, best moment ("Forty marbles roll down a spiral track and drop one by one into a grid until the last one completes a perfect smiley").
Not a story: no characters, no dialogue, no plot twist. One place (a table top, a studio floor, a white void).
<!-- /ideas -->

<!-- script -->
Format: a satisfying AI-made video. Entertainment: no selling, no product, no business.
- No narrator. Set every shot's "line" to empty. The sound is the star: give every shot a precise sound cue in "sound" (a soft click, a glassy tap, sand hiss, a deep thunk, a satisfying pop) that lands on the moment of the fit.
- 10 to 14 shots, 2 to 3 seconds each. Each shot is ONE motion that starts, runs smoothly and resolves on a clear "fit" moment the drawing can show (the piece slides in flush, the last ball drops, the stack squares off). Something resolves every 2 to 3 seconds.
- It escalates: each beat a little bigger, faster or more precise than the last, building to the biggest, cleanest fit in the last shot. The last frame matches the first so it loops.
- Cameras: mostly close-up, insert and high angle (straight down on the table), with a wide every few shots so the viewer sees the whole thing growing. Never the same camera twice in a row.
- On-screen text: at most 3 words, and only where it adds tension ("ONE LEFT", "PERFECT FIT?"). Most shots have none.
- "place" is the one surface it all happens on (the table top). The "story" field says in two sentences what process the viewer watches and how it ends.
- The caption says it was made with AI ("Made with AI") and asks one light question ("Which one was the most satisfying?"), plus 3 to 5 hashtags including #satisfying.
<!-- /script -->
