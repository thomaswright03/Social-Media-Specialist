# Format: product ad (20% of Reels)

One product from wright-ai-solutions.com, and Wright AI Solutions itself: we
build custom AI agents and software for businesses.

<!-- ideas -->
Format: an ad. Market ONE product (below) and Wright AI Solutions as a whole: we build custom AI agents and software for businesses.
What wins: a tiny story about one business owner with a painful, specific problem the product fixes (a missed call that cost a job, a pile of invoices at midnight), shown fast, then the product fixing it in a way that looks almost unbelievable. It must entertain first: a viewer who never buys still enjoys watching.
A pitch is one sentence with the owner, the problem and the moment the product saves them.
<!-- /ideas -->

<!-- script -->
Format: an ad. Market ONE product (below) and Wright AI Solutions as a whole: we build custom AI agents and software for businesses. Name no other product.
- A narrator speaks from the first second (the first shot has a line) and in nearly every shot, in the owner's voice or as a warm storyteller.
- Structure: the problem in the first 3 seconds (mid-action, felt, specific), the cost of it, the product doing its most impressive real feature on screen, the result (time or money saved, said as a real number only if the product's page says it), then "we can build one for your business too", and the call to action: link in bio.
- 10 to 14 shots, 2 to 3 seconds each. Each shot is ONE clear action a drawing shows mid-move. The product's screen is shown big and readable in at least 2 shots (an insert of the phone or laptop with the feature working).
- Cameras: wide, medium, close-up, over-the-shoulder, low or high angle, insert. Never the same camera twice in a row, at least 3 kinds; use an insert for the product's screen.
- Only claims the product's page makes. No fake reviews, no invented numbers, no "#1".
- The caption names the product, says we build custom AI agents and software for businesses, and ends with "Link in bio", plus 3 to 5 hashtags.
<!-- /script -->

A bright, confident flat-vector film still. Characters are white stick figures (stroke #f5f5f5, stroke-width 12 to 18, round caps, simple expressive faces) in a real workplace drawn edge to edge with depth (a truck yard, an office, a kitchen, a shop floor), lit warmly from a window or overhead lights, with soft shadows. Screens (phones, laptops) are drawn big, clean and readable: a white or dark UI card with rounded corners, the feature shown as simple blocks, icons and short labels at least 48 px. Palette: dark slate backgrounds (#111827 to #334155), warm light, lime #a3e635 for the product's success moment and buttons, red #ef4444 only for the problem (a missed call, an error).
