# Stage 7: Test small

Post 2 or 3 versions of the same video with different hooks (only the first
3 seconds and the caption's first line differ) as Trial Reels, which Instagram
shows only to non-followers first. Graduation to followers stays manual.

**Gate (code, `gateTest`), after 48 hours:**
- every version has been up 48 hours and reached at least 100 accounts
  (otherwise it waits, up to 7 days, then says the test was too small);
- the winner is picked by 3-second hold (1 - skip rate), average watch %
  and shares per reach: the version that ranks best across the three.
